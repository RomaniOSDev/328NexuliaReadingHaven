## Platform

ios

## Product Purpose

Nexulia Reading Haven is a personal reading journal for writing and revisiting chapter synopses and draft notes.

## Operating Context

The Library provides search and sorting. Drafts can be linked to a synopsis or promoted into one. Overview highlights recent edits. Stats summarizes the journal and its activity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing app targets iPhone and persists entries on the device. The current interface language is English.
