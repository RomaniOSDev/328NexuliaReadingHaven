# Nexulia Reading Haven

The journal takes its colors from the app icon. Deep indigo is the reading surface, pale lilac is the text, and orchid marks actions and focus. Pink and blue echo the icon in the masthead, illustrations, and charts.

Nexulia Press is an original display face drawn for Latin capitals. It sets the masthead and section titles. Controls, editable text, and reading paragraphs use the iOS system face and follow Dynamic Type.

The library and drafts have separate linocut illustrations for their empty states. Lists let the book title lead and use fine rules between entries. Charts use the same inks and retain direct labels.

Primary buttons use a violet ink texture. Search, title, and writing fields share a textured indigo surface with an orchid focus border. Labels, inputs, pickers, and navigation use native SwiftUI controls.

## Image generation

The asset catalog contains the four ImageGen outputs used by the app.

- **LibraryBookplate** uses the open book cutout with linocut hatching, pale lavender pages, saturated violet and indigo outlines, and a raspberry ribbon. The icon supplied the palette. The cutout retains its alpha.
- **DraftBookplate** shows two opaque pale lavender index cards, a cobalt paper clip, and a raspberry pencil in a woodblock print. The cutout has a transparent background and the card faces are unmarked.
- **ButtonInk** is a flat macro scan of matte orchid ink pressed into uncoated paper, with even color and subtle speckling. The ink sits under dark native labels.
- **FieldPaper** is an even macro scan of dark indigo bookcloth with faint fibers. Low contrast keeps editable text and placeholders legible.
